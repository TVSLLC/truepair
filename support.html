Common questions

How do I start scoring?
Tap +, name the event, add shooters and squads in Setup, then tap Start event. Score from the Score tab.

I tapped HIT by mistake.
Tap Undo last target, or tap any numbered target to change it to HIT or MISS.

Can I change target counts during a shoot?
Yes. Counts apply to every shooter and can't go below the most targets already scored at that station.

How do I move my events to a new phone?
On the Events screen, tap ⋯ then Back up all events and save the file. On the new phone, tap ⋯ then Restore from backup. Restoring adds events that aren't already there and never overwrites existing ones.

Does True Pair sync between devices?
Not yet. Use Back up and Restore to move events.

Does it enforce NSCA or FITASC rules?
No. True Pair records scores; the scorer applies shooting order, no-bird, rethrow and penalty decisions.

Can I use light mode?
Yes. Tap ⋯ on the Events screen and choose Light, Dark or System.
