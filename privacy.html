TRUE PAIR

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

True Pair is a sporting clays scorecard app made by Michael Hardwick. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

We don't collect your data

True Pair does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Events, courses, shooter names and scores you enter are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

When you share something

If you choose to share a results CSV or a backup file, it goes only where you send it, such as Files, AirDrop, Mail or Messages. That sharing is handled by iOS and the app you pick.

Deleting your data

You can delete any event in the app. Deleting True Pair removes all of its data from your device. Keep a backup file if you want to restore your events later.

Children

True Pair does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
